Privacy Policy

This application is a personal finance dashboard used only by its owner.

It may access the owner's bank account information through Enable Banking for the purpose of displaying balances, transactions, expenses and personal financial information.

The application is not offered to third parties and does not sell or share personal financial data for commercial purposes.

Bank authentication is handled by the relevant bank and Enable Banking. The application does not store bank login credentials.

Financial data may be stored locally or in infrastructure controlled by the application owner for personal use.
